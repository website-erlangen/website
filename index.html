Erlangen

Oncology clinical trials at community hospitals.

Most people with cancer in the US are treated close to home. But many community hospitals have no research program of their own, so their patients rarely get the chance to join a clinical trial, including trials matched to the genetics of their tumor.

Erlangen acts as the research site for these hospitals. We take on the regulatory work, coordination and investigator responsibilities, so trials can run where patients are already treated, under the care of their own oncologist.

Oncologists

Offer your patients clinical trials while they stay in your care.

Hospitals

Run clinical trials without building a research office.

Sponsors

Reach eligible patients at community hospitals outside existing research networks.

Investors

We are building the research layer for the hospitals where most cancer care happens.
