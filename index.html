ERLANGEN

The infrastructure for precision oncology.

Erlangen is a centralized site of record that enables hospitals of any size to run more oncology clinical trials.

Patients gain access to the most advanced therapies while continuing their care with their own oncologist. Hospitals and physicians can offer clinical trials, with Erlangen taking care of the regulatory and operational work.

For pharmaceutical partners, we provide a single point of access to eligible patients across hospitals, helping trials of increasingly personalized therapies reach full enrollment.
